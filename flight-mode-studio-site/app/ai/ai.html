Three ways we help teams use AI

Start with one or combine them. Every engagement is scoped after a free readiness call, so you only pay for what moves the needle.

AI automation

For teams buried in repetitive reporting, admin and content operations.

  • We map your workflows and find the tasks AI can take over safely
  • We build automations and AI agents that connect to the tools you already use, like your CRM, email, spreadsheets and ad platforms
  • Humans stay in the loop wherever a decision matters
  • We document everything and support it after launch

AI readiness & training

For leadership teams who need a plan before they roll AI out.

  • A readiness assessment of your teams, processes, tools and data
  • A practical AI policy covering what's allowed, what isn't, and how to handle data under POPIA
  • A prioritised roadmap of quick wins and longer-term projects
  • Workshops that get managers and staff confident with AI in their real work

Claude training

For teams adopting Claude who want people using it well from day one.

  • Hands-on sessions built around your team's actual tasks, not generic demos
  • Writing, research, analysis and document work with Claude
  • Setting up shared projects, instructions and reusable workflows for your team
  • Guidance on safe use, review habits and what to keep out of prompts

Questions

Do we need technical people on our side?

No. We handle the technical build. We need someone who knows the process we're automating, and a sponsor who can make decisions.

Is our data safe?

We design every automation and training session around what data can and can't go into AI tools, and we help you put a written policy in place that respects POPIA and your existing IT rules.

How long does it take?

A readiness assessment usually takes one to two weeks. Training can start within days. Automation projects depend on scope and we'll give you a timeline in your plan.

Which AI tools do you work with?

We work mostly with Claude and connect it to the tools your business already uses. If your team is set on another tool, we'll tell you honestly whether it suits the job.

What does it cost?

It depends on team size and scope. After the free call you'll get a fixed quote for the assessment, training or build, with no open-ended retainers.
