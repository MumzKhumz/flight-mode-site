FAQ

Questions before takeoff?

Who is this for?
Flight Mode Studio is built for small businesses, e-commerce brands, coaches, creators, and growing brands who need consistent video content without the cost of traditional production.
How fast do I get my videos?
Most videos are delivered within 3–5 business days of receiving your brief. No long waits, no scheduling chaos.
Is everything AI-generated?
No. We use AI to speed up production, but every video is reviewed, edited, and polished by a human before delivery. The best of both worlds.
Can I use the videos for ads?
Yes — all plans include ad-ready formats optimised for paid social on Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook.
Can I request a bigger campaign?
Yes. Campaign bursts are available for product launches, paid ad campaigns, and seasonal content. Pricing starts at R30k.
Can I cancel or pause?
Absolutely. You can pause or cancel your subscription at any time with no penalty. Total flexibility, no lock-in.
