Sportscene TVC

A visual story that disrupted a one-way flow of culture.

With Amapiano breaking globally, we built a TVC around the story of South African artists pioneering the shift. Dj Doowap's "Déjà Vu" anchored the spot — playing on the feeling of having seen something before, while showing little Doowap dreaming of the stages she'd one day share with Diplo.

The film was shot largely in Europe — historically positioned as the centre of the arts. We flipped the framing: the global south as creator, not consumer. A unilateral cultural flow, disrupted in 60 seconds.
